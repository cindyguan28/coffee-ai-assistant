# Beanmemo SEO Growth Plan

Owner: Marketing / SEO  
Tracking issue: COF-39  
Primary market: English-language search  
Canonical domain: `https://beanmemo.com`

## Current baseline — 2026-09-10

- Google Search Console Domain Property verified by the owner.
- Google currently returns no indexed Beanmemo result for `site:beanmemo.com`.
- Homepage, `robots.txt`, `sitemap.xml`, and `/coffee-journal` all return HTTP 200.
- Robots rules allow the public site and reference the canonical sitemap.
- Sitemap contains the homepage, privacy page, and first SEO guide.
- Homepage and article have canonical metadata; the article has Article and FAQ structured data.

This is a normal pre-indexing state for a new domain. Verification proves ownership; it does not request or guarantee indexing.

## Immediate Search Console actions

Complete these once in Google Search Console:

1. Open **Sitemaps** for the `beanmemo.com` Domain Property.
2. Submit `https://beanmemo.com/sitemap.xml`.
3. Open **URL inspection** and inspect `https://beanmemo.com/`.
4. If the page is not indexed, click **Request indexing**.
5. Repeat URL inspection for `https://beanmemo.com/coffee-journal` and request indexing.
6. Check **Page indexing** after 3–7 days. Do not repeatedly request the same URL every day.

The first useful success signal is not a top ranking. It is: **Discovered → Crawled → Indexed → First impressions**.

## Positioning for organic search

Beanmemo should not try to rank first for the broad word `coffee`. The defendable search position is:

> A private, personal coffee journal that helps curious coffee drinkers remember beans and brews, then understand their own taste.

The differentiation is personal memory and taste discovery—not professional scoring, a public social feed, or a complicated brewing laboratory.

## First 30 days

### Week 1 — Discovery and measurement

- Submit the sitemap and request indexing for the homepage and `/coffee-journal`.
- Record a baseline in Search Console: indexed pages, impressions, clicks, average position, and queries.
- Share the guide from the founder’s existing channels only where it is genuinely relevant.
- Ask 3–5 early users what phrase they would search when trying to remember coffee beans or brew recipes.

### Week 2 — Publish the second search page

- Publish **Coffee Brew Log: What to Record and What to Skip**.
- Target `coffee brew log`, `coffee brewing log`, and `what to record when brewing coffee`.
- Link it naturally from `/coffee-journal`; link back to the journal guide.
- Include one pour-over and one espresso example using real field labels.

### Week 3 — Build topical depth

- Publish **How to Take Coffee Tasting Notes Without Pretending to Be an Expert**.
- Target `coffee tasting notes` and `how to describe coffee taste`.
- Explain liking versus sensory intensity using Beanmemo’s implemented model.
- Reference WCR and SCA without copying their protected content.

### Week 4 — Improve conversion from search

- Review Search Console queries and landing-page impressions.
- Rewrite title/meta only when actual query data suggests a clearer match.
- Add privacy-respecting conversion measurement for article → signup clicks.
- Interview at least three activated users about what made them start or stop logging.

## 90-day content cadence

Publish two strong pages per month. Quality and internal linking matter more than volume.

1. Coffee Brew Log: What to Record and What to Skip
2. How to Take Coffee Tasting Notes Without Pretending to Be an Expert
3. How to Find the Coffee You Actually Like
4. Coffee Bean Tracker: A Simple System for Your Home Shelf
5. Espresso Journal: Dose, Yield, Time, Taste, Next Step
6. Washed vs Natural Coffee: What to Notice in Your Own Cup

Every article must include an original Beanmemo example, a clear next step, relevant references, and links to at least two other public Beanmemo pages once available.

## Distribution without spam

- Founder channel: share the learning or template, not only the product link.
- Coffee communities: answer an existing question fully before mentioning Beanmemo; disclose the relationship.
- Early users: invite them to share anonymized journaling patterns or their own vocabulary.
- Roasters: pursue co-created educational examples only after the product has active users and a clear value exchange.
- Product directories: defer mass submissions until the positioning and onboarding conversion are stable.

Do not buy backlinks, automate low-quality guest posts, or create keyword-stuffed location pages.

## Weekly SEO scorecard

Review every Monday:

| Metric | Source | Why it matters |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Indexed public pages | Search Console | Google can surface the site |
| Search impressions | Search Console | Early discovery is growing |
| Non-brand queries | Search Console | Topic authority is forming |
| Organic clicks | Search Console | Listings earn visits |
| Article → signup clicks | First-party analytics | Content supports acquisition |
| Activated organic users | Product analytics | Traffic becomes real value |

For the first month, optimize for indexing and impressions. Do not judge SEO by signups before enough qualified impressions exist.

## Decision rules

- If a URL is not indexed after 14 days, inspect the Search Console reason before changing content.
- If indexed but receiving no impressions after 30 days, reassess the query and title against the actual search results.
- If impressions rise but click-through stays below 1% after at least 200 impressions, test a clearer title and meta description.
- If clicks arrive but signups do not, improve message match and CTA before creating more content.
- Never claim that a ranking or indexing date is guaranteed.
